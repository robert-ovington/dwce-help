Clocking In & Out

Use the Clock In / Clock Out button to record when you arrive at and leave a work site. Your location is captured at the time of clocking to confirm site attendance.

Clocking In

Clock in as soon as you arrive on site at the start of your working day or shift.

  1. At the top of the Main Menu, tap the large Clock In card. This opens the clock-in pop-down over the menu — you do not navigate to a separate screen.
  2. The app will read your current GPS location. Ensure you are on or near the work site before proceeding.
  3. Select the project you are working on (see Selecting a Project below).
  4. Tap the Clock In button. A confirmation dialog appears summarising the project, client, time, and distance from the job — check the details and tap the coloured confirm button to finish, or Cancel to go back.
  5. A brief "Clocked in successfully!" message appears at the bottom of the screen once confirmed.
✅

Clock in before you start any work, not at the end of the day. The time recorded is the exact moment you tap the button (unless you have adjusted it — see Adjusting the Clock Time).

Clocking Out

Clock out when you are about to leave the site at the end of your working day or shift.

  1. At the top of the Main Menu, tap the Clock Out card (it replaces the Clock In card once you are clocked in) to open the same pop-down.
  2. Make sure you are still on site before tapping.
  3. Tap Clock Out. A confirmation dialog appears with the same summary details — check them and confirm.
  4. A brief "Clocked out successfully!" message appears once confirmed.
⚠️

Do not clock out and then continue working. Clock out only when you are genuinely finishing for the day.

Recording a Break

If breaks are enabled for your account, a Breaks section appears in the clock-in pop-down. Tap Add Break to log a start and finish time for an unpaid break taken during the day. Any breaks you have added are shown in the confirmation dialog when you clock in or out.

Selecting a Project

You can record which project or job you are clocking in for. The clock-in pop-down provides two shortcuts to choose a project, plus manual search:

Find Nearest
Uses your current GPS location to find the closest active project. Tap the button and the app will show the nearest match. If there are multiple nearby sites, the button label changes so you can tap it again to cycle through the next closest options.
Find Last
Opens a list of recent projects you have worked on. Tap any entry to fill in the project field instantly without searching.
Type to search
Tap the project field and start typing part of the project name. Matching results will appear as you type.

Adjusting the Clock Time

By default the clocking screen uses the current time. If you need to record a slightly earlier or later time (for example, if you were unable to clock in immediately on arrival), you can adjust the time in 15-minute steps using the time selector on the screen.

⚠️

The actual time you tapped the button is also saved alongside the manually adjusted time. Only adjust the time if there is a genuine reason — do not use it to alter your records.

Vehicle Selection

If your role records vehicle usage, a Vehicles section appears in the clock-in pop-down showing your saved fleet vehicles (up to six, set up by the office). These are listed automatically — there is nothing to select. If a listed vehicle is wrong, tap the delete (bin) icon next to it to remove it from this clock-in. You can also add a vehicle manually if it is not in your saved list.

ℹ️

The Vehicles section only appears if it has been enabled for your account. If it is not visible and you think it should be, contact the office.

My Clockings

My Clockings shows a read-only history of your clock-in and clock-out records grouped by date, with your total hours for each day. Use it to confirm a clocking was recorded correctly.

Location Permission Required

The Clocking screen requires your phone's GPS to be active. If the app cannot read your location, the Clock In / Out button will be disabled or an error will appear.

Checklist if location is not working

  • Make sure your phone's Location / GPS is switched on in your phone's settings
  • Check that the DWCE Time Tracker app has been granted location permission — go to Settings → Apps → DWCE Time Tracker → Permissions → Location → Allow While Using App
  • Stand in an open area away from buildings if possible — GPS accuracy improves in open spaces
  • Wait a few seconds and try again — GPS can take a moment to get a fix

Missed a Clocking

If you forgot to clock in or out, you cannot add a missed clocking yourself through the app. Report the missed clocking to your supervisor who can amend your attendance record from the admin side of the system.

ℹ️

Your timesheet (added separately) is not the same as your clocking record. You can still submit a timesheet for a day even if the clocking record needs to be corrected.
