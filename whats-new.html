What's New

A running summary of recent updates and improvements to the DWCE Time Tracker app, newest first.

23 August 2026

  • Offline PIN unlock (new) — set a 6-digit PIN and "Sign Out" becomes Lock instead of a full sign-out, so you can get straight back into the app with no signal by entering your PIN rather than needing a connection to log in again. You'll be prompted to set one up the first time you log in after this update, or set/change it any time from your account menu ("Set/Change Offline PIN"). Getting the PIN wrong 5 times locks it for 30 seconds. "Sign out completely" is still there on the PIN screen for when that's genuinely what you want.
  • Fixed the app getting stuck on an endless spinner when reopened with no signal (e.g. Aeroplane Mode) with a previous session still on the device — it now restores straight into the app instead.
  • The Close App button (Login screen, Android) now properly closes the app every time — it could previously still show up in your recent-apps list looking like it hadn't fully closed.
  • Timesheet Completion (Dashboard, supervisors/admins) now also flags a day where a clock-in/out doesn't quite add up, and lists anyone who hasn't clocked in on a weekday that's already finished, so it can be caught sooner rather than at payroll.
  • Working Offline — if something fails to sync, you'll now see a clear red notice on-screen as soon as it happens, even once you're back online, instead of it sitting invisibly until someone happens to open Offline Status.

23 August 2026 — Behind the Scenes

  • Fixed a Daylight Saving-related bug where editing a timesheet entry could log a false "changed" entry in its history for a time that hadn't actually changed.
  • Continued work on making sure nothing gets duplicated if an offline save has to retry after a poor signal. No visible change to how the app works.

22 August 2026

  • Beacon scanning is now manual — for privacy, the app no longer scans for Bluetooth plant beacons automatically in the background. A Scan for Beacons button on the Asset Check screen (and Beacon Trial) starts a scan on demand instead.
  • Quick Clock In — clock times and break start/end now always snap to the nearest 15 minutes, matching the time picker, so daily and weekly totals stay accurate.
  • Fixed My Clockings missing from the Main Menu on the Web version (it was already there on mobile).
  • You can now delete your own Float Day, Sick Day, Appointment and Extra Hours entries from the current week in Time Off — previously only an admin could.
  • Renamed My Clockings to Clocking History on the Main Menu, with My Clockings and Time Off now grouped underneath it.

21 August 2026

  • Time Off (new) — staff with "Time Off Entitlement" enabled can now record time off directly from Quick Clock In. Tap + Time Off for a Float Day or Sick Day — pick a From/To date range and every weekday (Monday to Friday) in that range is recorded as a separate day — or an Appointment, where you pick the date and set how long you were out using the +/- buttons in 15-minute steps. Tap + Extra Hours to log time worked back against an appointment the same way.
  • Appointment time and Extra Hours worked back are tracked as a running Time Off Balance, shown on the Dashboard whenever it isn't zero.
  • Weekly Summary and Employee Summary now show "Float" or "Sick" instead of hours worked on days recorded as a Float or Sick Day, and highlight Appointment days in yellow so it's clear why the total is lower than usual.
  • Fixed the Previous/Next arrows above the employee name on Admin Staff Attendance not working when the Employee filter was set to a single person.
  • Admin Staff Attendance and Weekly Summary now only include active staff when navigating between employees or displaying all staff.
  • Added a Display All button to Weekly Summary (matching the one already on Admin Staff Attendance) to show every active office/admin staff member with clock-in enabled, including anyone who hasn't clocked in yet this week.

16 August 2026

  • Working Offline (new) — on the Android and iPhone app, Clock In/Out, Timesheets, Plant Checks, and Deliveries now keep working with no signal. Log in once with a connection and the app will let you straight in next time even with no signal; anything you do offline — including delivery and plant check photos — uploads automatically as soon as you're back in range, with nothing to press. See the new Working Offline guide for the full details, including what's available and what still needs a connection. This is an initial release — refinements based on real-world field testing will be listed in future updates.
  • Close App button — the Login screen (Android only) now has a red Close App button in the top-right corner that fully closes the app, including stopping any background beacon scanning, rather than just minimising it.
  • My Clockings on the Main Menu now correctly requires the Clock In permission, the same as the Clock In button itself.
  • The Create User screen now includes the same "Offline Access" options (Offline Clock In/Out, Offline Timesheets, Offline Plant Checks, Offline Deliveries) as Edit User, so they can be set correctly when a new account is created rather than only afterwards.

16 August 2026 — Behind the Scenes

  • Updated several of the app's underlying building blocks (file picker, secure credential storage, and device/permission handling) to their latest versions to keep the app current and secure. No visible change to how the app works.
  • Updated the Android build tools the app compiles with (Gradle, Android Gradle Plugin, Kotlin, and the Android SDK version) to keep pace with Google's requirements for future Play Store updates. No visible change to how the app works.

13 August 2026

  • "Multiple User" time periods — when entering a timesheet for other people, supervisors can now submit the same time period (date, time, breaks and project) to several employees from one employer at once, instead of adding them one at a time. The review screen blocks submission and shows a warning if any of the selected employees already has an overlapping time period that day.
  • Fixed times, breaks, saved fleet, allowances and job history not switching over properly when entering a time period "For Another User" — these now correctly reflect the other person, and revert back to your own when the option is unticked.
  • Dashboard — "My Clocking History" is now always shown in full (no more expand/collapse), and you can edit a clocking — including adding a missing finish time — directly from the Dashboard, the same as on the admin attendance screen.
  • Fixed the Dashboard being unreachable on some Android tablets.
  • Clocking out on a different calendar day to when you clocked in is now handled properly: you'll see a warning showing your total hours worked, and the record is automatically split into a clean entry for each day.
  • Quick Clock In — you can now pick the day (current week only, the same as Timesheets), and tapping the clock time opens a quick-set popup as a faster alternative to the +/- 15 minute buttons.
  • Weekly Summary & Employee Summary — the admin "Summary" screen has been renamed Weekly Summary, and a new Employee Summary screen has been added showing one employee's hours with one row per week, oldest first, for a chosen year.
  • Admin Staff Attendance — the Employee list now sorts A-Z, there's a new Export to CSV button, and a "Display All" filter that now works alongside the day filter instead of resetting it.
  • Asset Check — the action buttons now stay at the top of the screen so the scanned items list doesn't push them out of view.
  • Added the missing "Large Plant" permission to the Create User and Edit User screens and to bulk CSV user import.

9 August 2026

  • Create User improvements — the Create User screen now includes the same full set of menu permissions and user options as Edit User (Dashboard access, Clock In settings, Beacon Scanning, and more), and bulk CSV user imports now correctly apply every permission listed in the file instead of silently skipping some of them.
  • Fixed an issue where some employees were shown Holiday pay for days they had actually worked.
  • Fixed the Sign Out button on Android showing a blank screen instead of returning to the login screen.
  • Fixed the Asset Check screen showing NFC error messages when used on a computer (web version) — it now goes straight to Manual Entry as expected, since computers don't have an NFC scanner.
  • Comments added to a time period now appear directly under the relevant entry on the Paper Timesheet, shown in bold red, both on-screen and in the printed/PDF version.

4 August 2026

  • Terms & Conditions updated — the Terms & Conditions now define "DWCE", "the Company" and "the DWCE Group" as covering Fellonwood Ltd., David Walsh Civil Engineering Ltd. and Walsh Plant & Equipment Limited. The app itself continues to be referred to as DWCE Time Tracker.

23 July 2026

  • Stock Locations Management now covers Large Plant — the Stock Locations Management screen has been split into two tabs, "Sites" and "Large Plant". The new Large Plant tab lists every piece of plant (sorted by plant number) with a quick search box and a filter by plant type, plus a toggle on each row to mark that plant as a valid stock location for deliveries and asset checks.

15 July 2026

  • Create Project (new) — Office and admin staff can now create a new project directly in the app for the rare occasions a job needs to be opened before the Access-managed project list can be updated (e.g. weekends, shutdowns). The screen walks you through picking the right client, contract, and location — with GPS and a tap-to-pick map, and automatic County/Town lookup — then shows a full preview before the project is created.
  • Fixed an issue where creating a Work Order from a reported prestart fault could fail to save.

13–14 July 2026

  • Large Plant Hub redesigned — plant details, service history, alerts, and open jobs are now all visible on one scrollable screen instead of being split across tabs. Tap the Plant Due Soon / Plant with Alerts tiles to filter the plant list.
  • Fixed the "Last used by" information on the Large Plant Hub to correctly show the driver/operator, rather than a mechanic's workshop time.
  • Fixed the current clock reading occasionally not appearing on the Large Plant Hub.
  • Fixed a crash that could occur when leaving the Dashboard while clocking history was still loading.
  • Fixed mechanic names failing to load on the Service Register plant detail screen.

30 June – 2 July 2026

  • Added a live clock display above the adjustable time in Quick Clock In.
  • Improved the "My Clocking History" dashboard card with break times and weekly totals.
  • Fixed timesheets briefly showing absence-only days in approval screens where they shouldn't appear.
  • Fixed clock-in/out times occasionally showing 1 hour earlier than they should.
