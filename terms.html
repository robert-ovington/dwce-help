About this app

DWCE Time Tracker is DWCE's internal system for recording time and attendance, project and fleet allocation, plant and asset checks, deliveries, PPE requests and workplace messaging. It is provided for use by DWCE employees and authorised contractors in the course of their work, on either a company or personal mobile device, or through the Web version at a desktop or office computer.

ℹ️

These Terms & Conditions are shown in the app whenever a new version is published. You must accept the current version to continue using the app — see Version history & acceptance below.

Acceptable use

  • Use the app only for genuine work purposes and enter accurate, honest records.
  • Keep your login credentials private. Do not share your account, or clock in or out on behalf of another person.
  • Report faults, damage or safety issues promptly using the relevant module (Plant Checks, Deliveries, Messages).
  • Photos, signatures and check results you submit become part of the company's official records and should reflect what actually happened on site.
⚠️

Misuse of the app — including falsifying clock times, plant checks or delivery records — is treated as a disciplinary matter in the same way as falsifying a paper record.

GPS & location data — App vs Web

This is the one area where the mobile app and the Web version of DWCE Time Tracker behave differently, and it's important you understand which one applies to you.

📱
Mobile App (Android / iOS)
Your GPS location is recorded at the moment you clock in or out, and when you use location-assisted features such as Find Nearest. The app does not track your location in the background outside of these actions.
💻
Web Version
The Web version does not request, access or record GPS location under any circumstance — browsers accessing the Web app are never asked for location permission. Clocking and timesheet entries made on the Web have no location attached.

See the Getting Started guide for the full breakdown of every mobile feature that uses your location, and the GDPR & Your Privacy section for how location and other personal data is protected, retained and who can see it.

Other data we collect

Beyond location, the app records the data it needs to run payroll, meet health & safety obligations and manage company equipment:

  • Identity data: your name and work email address
  • Attendance data: clock-in and clock-out times
  • Timesheet data: hours worked, project or vehicle allocated, equipment used, allowances claimed, and any comments you add
  • Inspection data: results of plant pre-start checks and asset checks, including any fault photos
  • Delivery data: aggregate and waste delivery details, including captured signatures
  • Messaging data: messages sent to you and read-receipts for important messages

Full detail on why this is collected, the legal basis, retention periods and who can see it is covered in the GDPR & Your Privacy section of the Getting Started guide — it applies equally whether you use the app or the Web version.

Version history & acceptance

These Terms & Conditions may be updated from time to time — for example, if a new feature changes what data is collected. Whenever a new version is published, you will be asked to review and accept it the next time you log in before you can continue. Declining signs you out; you can accept the next time you log in.

Older versions are never edited or deleted — each published version is kept exactly as it was accepted, so there is always an accurate record of what you agreed to and when.

✅

On the Web version of the app, the acceptance screen includes a "View revision history" link. This opens a list of every version ever published, and tapping any older version opens it on its own archived page so you can read exactly what an earlier version said.

Questions & your rights

If you have questions about these terms, or want to exercise any of your data protection rights (access, correction, erasure, objection), contact your supervisor or the DWCE office. All requests are handled within 30 days. Data protection in Ireland is overseen by the Data Protection Commission (DPC) — see dataprotection.ie for more information.
