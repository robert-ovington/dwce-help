Plant Checks

Plant checks ensure equipment is safe and fit for use before and during operation. This section covers pre-start checks for large plant, asset checks for small plant, and how to report faults.

Large Plant

Large plant refers to heavy machinery and vehicles — excavators, dumpers, rollers, lorries, and similar equipment. Before operating any large plant, a pre-start check must be completed. The first time you check a particular machine the app will walk you through a one-off setup before starting the inspection.

a. Selecting a Machine

  1. From the main menu, tap Plant Checks → Large Plant.
  2. Type part of the machine number or description in the search field to filter the list.
  3. Tap the machine you want to check.

Check Saved Plant

If you regularly operate the same machines, tap Check Saved Plant to open a shortcut list of up to six vehicles saved to your profile. Tap any entry to go straight to that machine's check without searching.

ℹ️

Your saved fleet vehicles are set up by the office. If you need them updated, contact your supervisor.

b. First-Time Setup

The first time you select a machine that has not been configured before, the app runs a one-off setup. This only needs to be done once per machine. Future checks will skip straight to the inspection.

  1. Intro — a brief overview of the setup process is shown. Tap Start Setup to begin.
  2. Clock / Odometer — confirm whether this machine has a clock, hour-meter, or odometer. If yes, select the unit (Hours, Kilometres, or Miles).
  3. Questions — work through the inspection questions one by one. For each question, tap:
    • Pass — the item is applicable and currently in good condition
    • Fault — the item is applicable but has a defect (you will be asked to describe it)
    • N/A — the question does not apply to this machine (it will be hidden in all future checks for this machine)
  4. Review — confirm the setup summary and tap Save Configuration.

The setup responses also count as your first inspection. Any faults you record during setup are submitted immediately.

✅

Marking a question as N/A removes it permanently from future checks for that machine. Only use N/A if the question genuinely cannot apply (for example, a "trailer coupling" question on a machine that has no trailer).

c. Submitting a Pre-Start Check

A pre-start check must be completed before you operate a piece of large plant each day.

  1. Select the machine (see Selecting a Machine above).
  2. The Details screen appears. Confirm the date and, if the machine has a clock, enter the current reading.
    • If a reading was recorded within the past 7 days, the app will pre-fill the field with that value as a starting point — update it to the current reading before continuing.
  3. Work through the checklist items one by one. For each item, tap:
    • Pass — item is in good condition
    • Fault — item has a defect (describe it and optionally add a photo)
  4. Only the questions marked as applicable during setup are shown — N/A questions are hidden.
  5. Once all items are answered, tap Submit Check.
⚠️

Do not rush through the checklist by tapping Pass on everything without physically checking each item. Pre-start checks are a legal safety requirement and must be completed honestly.

ℹ️

Your supervisor is notified automatically when a check is submitted with one or more faults.

d. Recording a Fault & Adding a Photo

If you find a defect or fault on a piece of large plant — before, during, or after a pre-start check — you must report it immediately.

  1. During a check, tap Fault on the relevant checklist item.
  2. Enter a description of the fault — be as specific as possible.
  3. To add a photo, tap Add Photo:
    • Choose Take Photo to use your camera directly
    • Or choose Choose from Gallery to use an existing photo
  4. Tap Next to continue to the next checklist item.
⚠️

Do not operate a machine with a reported safety-critical fault until it has been cleared by the site supervisor or maintenance team. Submitting a fault report in the app does not mean the machine has been cleared for use.

✅

Photos are extremely helpful for the maintenance team. Photograph the fault clearly, close-up, in good light, and include a wider shot showing where on the machine the fault is located.

Small Plant

Small plant refers to hand tools, power tools, and smaller equipment — generators, compactors, vibrators, breakers, and similar items. Asset checks are carried out by location and confirm that equipment at that location is present and in acceptable condition.

a. Asset Check for a Location

An asset check is a location-based inspection. You select the site or storage area, and the app displays all items that should be present at that location.

  1. From the main menu, tap Plant Checks → Small Plant.
  2. Tap New Asset Check.
  3. Select the location you are checking from the dropdown list (e.g. a specific site, compound, or storage area).
  4. The app will display the list of items assigned to that location.
  5. For each item, indicate its status:
    • Present & OK — item is on site and in good condition
    • Present — Fault — item is on site but has a defect (you will be prompted to record the fault — see below)
    • Not Present — item is missing from the location
  6. Once all items are reviewed, tap Submit Check.
✅

Walk the location with your phone and physically check each item as you go rather than filling the form in from memory.

b. Recording a Fault & Adding a Photo

When you identify a fault on a small plant item during an asset check — or at any time — you can submit a fault report directly.

  1. From the Small Plant screen, tap Report Fault, or when completing an asset check, select Present — Fault on the relevant item.
  2. Select the equipment item with the fault (if not already selected).
  3. Select the fault type from the list.
  4. Enter a clear description of the fault.
  5. Tap Add Photo to take a photo or choose one from your gallery. A photo is strongly recommended.
  6. Tap Submit to send the fault report.
⚠️

Tag or set aside any equipment with a reported fault so colleagues know it should not be used until repaired or replaced.

My Checks

My Checks shows a history of all pre-start and asset checks you have submitted. Use it to confirm a check was recorded correctly or to review past results.

  1. From the main menu, tap Plant Checks → My Checks.
  2. A list of your submitted checks appears, ordered by date — most recent first.
  3. Tap any entry to view the full details of that check, including each checklist item result and any fault notes.

What the summary shows

  • Date and time the check was submitted
  • Machine or location checked
  • Overall result — all passed, or number of items failed
  • Individual item results and any fault descriptions or photos
ℹ️

My Checks is a read-only record. Submitted checks cannot be edited. If you submitted a check with an error, notify your supervisor.
