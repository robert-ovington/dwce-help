Dashboard

The Dashboard gives you a personalised at-a-glance view of your clocking status, timesheets, team activity, and — for workshop users — live Work Order summaries. You can choose which information cards appear and in what order. For full details on the Workshop section of the dashboard, see the Workshop page.

What the Dashboard Shows

The Dashboard is made up of cards (also called widgets), grouped into categories. Each card shows a specific piece of information — for example, your current clock-in status, this week's hours, or a list of recent timesheets.

The categories and cards available to you depend on your role. Common categories include:

Category Cards included
My Clocking Clock Status, My Clocking History
My Time Periods My Weekly Hours, Recent Timesheets
Team Clocking (managers) Project Team Clock-In, Not Clocked In, Forgotten Clock-Outs
Team Time Periods (managers) Team This Week, Timesheet Completion
Workshop (workshop role) Workshop Quick Actions, My Work Orders, Open Work Orders, WO Status Counts
Plant Checks (plant checks role) Plant Checks Today
ℹ️

Categories are turned on and off by an administrator based on your role. If a category you expect is missing, contact the office.

Customising Your Widgets

Within each category you can choose which cards appear and control the order they are displayed. Open the widget configuration screen by tapping the gear icon (⚙️) in the top-right corner of the Dashboard screen.

Turning a card on or off

  1. Tap the gear icon on the Dashboard to open My Dashboard Widgets.
  2. Your enabled categories are listed as expandable sections. Tap a section heading to expand it.
  3. Each card in the section has a toggle switch on the right. Switch it on to show the card on your dashboard, or off to hide it.
  4. Tap Save at the bottom of the screen to apply your changes.

Reordering cards within a category

  1. In the My Dashboard Widgets screen, expand the category you want to rearrange.
  2. Tap and hold the drag handle (≡ icon) on the right of a card row.
  3. Drag the row up or down to the position you want.
  4. Tap Save to apply the new order.
✅

Cards within a category are ordered independently of other categories. You can have the most important card at the top of each section.

Dashboard Preferences

Dashboard Preferences let you control which project and which team members appear in the Team category cards. Access preferences from the Dashboard settings menu.

Project

Team cards can be filtered to show only the people working on a specific project. The app will try to auto-detect your project from today's clock-in record and display it at the top of the screen.

If you need to view a different project, use the Project (manual override) dropdown to select any active project. This overrides the auto-detected value until you change it again.

Project Prefix Filter

If your company uses project numbers with different letter prefixes (for example, "A" for civil works and "B" for groundworks), you can filter the team cards to show only users whose project number starts with the selected letter(s).

  1. In Dashboard Preferences, find the Project Prefix Filter section.
  2. Tap one or more letter chips to select them. Selected chips are highlighted.
  3. Team cards will only show users whose current project number begins with the selected letter(s).
  4. Leave all chips unselected to show users from all projects.
  5. Tap Save Preferences to apply.

Role Filter

You can narrow team cards down to specific employee roles — for example, to see only Operatives or only Supervisors.

  1. In Dashboard Preferences, find the Role Filter section.
  2. Tap the roles you want to include. Selected roles are highlighted.
  3. Leave all roles unselected to show all roles.
  4. Tap Save Preferences to apply.
ℹ️

Project Prefix Filter and Role Filter only affect the Team Clocking and Team Time Periods cards. Your own clocking and timesheet cards are not affected.
