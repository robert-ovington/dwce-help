Privacy Policy

This page explains what personal data DWCE Time Tracker collects, why, how long it is kept, who can see it, and the rights you have over it — for both the mobile app and the Web version.

  • Why we collect it & legal basis
  • How long data is kept

  • Your rights

Who this policy covers

DWCE Time Tracker is an internal system operated by the DWCE Group — Fellonwood Ltd., David Walsh Civil Engineering Ltd. and Walsh Plant & Equipment Limited — for use by DWCE employees and authorised contractors only. It is not a public consumer app and is not intended for use by, or collection of data from, the general public or children. Access requires a company-issued account.

Location data — App vs Web

📱
Mobile App (Android / iOS)
Your GPS location is recorded at the moment you clock in or out, and when you use location-assisted features such as Find Nearest. The app does not track your location in the background outside of these actions, and never collects location when the app is closed.
💻
Web Version
The Web version does not request, access or record GPS location under any circumstance — browsers accessing the Web app are never asked for location permission.

What other data is collected

  • Identity data: your name and work email address
  • Attendance data: clock-in and clock-out times
  • Timesheet data: hours worked, project or vehicle allocated, equipment used, allowances claimed, and any comments you add
  • Inspection data: results of plant pre-start checks and asset checks, including any fault photos you take
  • Delivery data: aggregate and waste delivery details, including captured signatures
  • Messaging data: messages sent to you and read-receipts for important messages

DWCE Time Tracker does not collect financial account details, health/fitness data, contacts, browsing history, or any data for advertising purposes. The app contains no ads and no third-party ad or analytics trackers.

Why we collect it & legal basis

  • To calculate your pay accurately (timesheet and attendance data)
  • To meet legal obligations for health and safety record-keeping (plant checks)
  • To fulfil contractual obligations with clients (delivery records)
  • To manage company assets and equipment effectively

The lawful basis for processing this data is the performance of your employment contract (Article 6(1)(b) EU GDPR). Some health and safety records are also processed to comply with a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) EU GDPR).

How long data is kept

Employment and payroll records are retained for a minimum of 6 years following the end of your employment, in line with Revenue Commissioners requirements. Health and safety records may be retained for longer periods where required by law.

Who can see your data

  • Your direct supervisor and site manager (attendance, timesheets, checks)
  • The payroll team (timesheet and allowance data)
  • Company management (aggregated reports)
  • DWCE's secure cloud database provider — data is stored in an encrypted database and is not shared with any third party for marketing or commercial purposes

How your data is protected

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is restricted by row-level security so staff can only see data relevant to their role. Accounts are individual and password protected; do not share your login.

Your rights

Under EU GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data held about you
  • Correct any inaccurate data
  • Erasure of data in certain circumstances (note: some records must be kept by law)
  • Object to processing where you believe it is not justified
ℹ️

Data protection in Ireland is overseen by the Data Protection Commission (DPC). For more information visit dataprotection.ie.

Questions & contact

To exercise any of your data rights, or ask questions about this policy, contact your supervisor or the DWCE office. All requests are handled within 30 days.
