Working Offline

On the Android and iPhone app (not the computer/web version), a set of core screens keep working even when you have no signal — clocking in/out, timesheets, plant checks and deliveries. This page explains what works, how it works, and what to do with anything left waiting to sync.

What Works Offline

These screens will keep working with no signal:

  • Clock In / Clock Out, including breaks, and My Clockings
  • Timesheets — New Entry (for yourself only, see below), My Timesheet, My Hours
  • Time Off — you can still view your existing requests (adding a new request needs a connection)
  • Large Plant Prestart Check — for a plant you've checked recently (see below)
  • Small Plant (Asset Check) and My Checks
  • Deliveries — Aggregates, Waste, and My Deliveries, including docket photos and signatures

Everything else in the app (Concrete Mix, Workshop, office/admin screens, and so on) needs a live connection, the same as before.

ℹ️

Offline mode is only available on the Android and iPhone app. The computer/web version always needs a connection.

ℹ️

Each of these areas has to be switched on for your account by the office (Clock In/Out, Timesheets, Plant Checks and Deliveries are each turned on separately). If a screen you expect to work offline doesn't, ask your supervisor or the office to check your account settings.

Logging In With No Signal

As long as you've logged in on your phone before, opening the app with no signal takes you straight to the Main Menu instead of getting stuck on the login screen. A banner at the top tells you you're working offline and shows when your data last synced.

✅

If you know you'll be heading somewhere with no signal, open the app and log in normally at least once beforehand — this is what lets your phone remember you.

Using the App Offline

For the screens listed above, you can carry on as normal — clock in/out, add breaks, fill in your timesheet, run plant checks, and log deliveries with photos. Everything you enter is saved on your phone first.

Timesheets — yourself only
While offline, New Entry only lets you record time for yourself. Recording time for someone else ("For Another User" or "Multiple User") needs a connection, even if you normally have permission to do it.
Large Plant Prestart Check — recently checked plant only
A plant's checklist is only available offline if you've run a prestart check on it in the current or previous week. If you try a plant you haven't recently checked, the app will tell you it's unavailable offline — run one check on it while you still have signal to make it available.
Small Plant scans
Scans are accepted straight away offline. If anything doesn't quite match once it syncs back to the office system, it's flagged for review rather than blocking you in the field.

Syncing Back Up

As soon as your phone finds a signal again, everything you did offline uploads automatically in the background — there's no button to press. Photos attached to deliveries or plant checks upload shortly after the record itself, so nothing you did is held up waiting on a photo.

Checking What's Outstanding

Look for the sync icon with a number badge in the top bar of the Main Menu — it shows how many items are still waiting to sync. Tap it to open the Offline Status screen:

  • Sync Queue tab — everything you've entered that hasn't synced yet, with a Sync Now button if you don't want to wait for it to happen automatically.
  • Reference Data tab — shows when the information cached on your phone (like your recent timesheets and plant checks) was last refreshed.

On list screens like My Clockings, My Timesheet, My Checks and My Deliveries, anything still waiting to sync is shown with a small orange "Pending sync" tag.

What You Can and Can't Edit Offline

You can freely edit or cancel something you entered while offline, right up until it's synced — look for the "Pending sync" tag. Once an entry is confirmed synced to the office system, changing it again needs a connection.

⚠️

This is deliberate — it keeps things simple and avoids two people's changes clashing. If you need to correct something that's already synced and you have no signal, make a note and correct it once you're back online, or ask your supervisor.

Why Is a Screen Greyed Out?

When you're offline, any Main Menu item that doesn't support offline use is shown greyed out with a "no connection" icon. Tapping it shows a message explaining it needs a connection — this is expected, not a fault. It'll become tappable again as soon as you're back in signal.

ℹ️

Can't find something you're looking for, or think something's not working right? Contact your supervisor or site manager for assistance.
