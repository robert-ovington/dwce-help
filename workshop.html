Workshop

The Workshop module lets mechanics conduct plant inspections, raise and manage Work Orders, track parts and materials, and view the service register. Access is role-controlled — contact your administrator if any section is missing from your menu.

Accessing Workshop

From the Main Menu, tap Workshop to expand the section. The following items are available to workshop-role users:

Menu item What it does
Inspection Report Conduct a structured inspection on a piece of plant
Inspection Log Browse and review previously submitted inspection reports
Work Orders View, claim, and complete job cards for plant repairs
Vehicle Parts Lists View and manage named parts lists per vehicle
Service Register Track scheduled maintenance and CVRT due dates for all plant
ℹ️

A separate Workshop Admin section appears for users with the Workshop Admin role. This covers inspection configuration, WO templates, inventory management, and fleet setup — see your administrator for guidance on those screens.

Inspection Report

Use this to carry out a formal plant inspection. The questions you are asked depend on the inspection configuration chosen — a configuration is a template that defines which questions apply to a particular type of vehicle or plant.

Starting an inspection

  1. Tap Workshop → Inspection Report.
  2. Select the plant item you are inspecting from the "Select Plant" dropdown.
  3. Select an Inspection Template from the "Select Inspection Template" dropdown (e.g. "Road Vehicle — Full" or "Large Plant — Daily").
  4. Tap Start Inspection. If the template requires a clock reading, a dialog prompts you for it at this point.
  5. If there are previously reported Monitor items still unresolved for this plant, a popup lists them — these will auto-resolve if the same questions are answered Pass during this inspection.

Completing inspection questions

Questions are shown one section at a time. Each question may have one of the following answer types:

  • Check — Pass / Monitor / Fault (tap to select).
  • Text — Type a free-text answer.
  • Numeric / Percentage — Enter a measured value.
  • List — Select from a predefined list of options.

Mark a question as Fault if it requires repair. Mark as Monitor if it needs watching but does not require immediate action. You can add a comment and attach a photo to any question.

Resuming a draft

If you start an inspection and leave before submitting, it is saved as a draft. When you next open Inspection Report, any drafts in progress are shown at the top of the screen. Tap a draft to resume where you left off.

Submitting

When all questions are answered, tap Submit. The report is saved with any questions marked as Fault or Monitor recorded against the plant — linking them to a Work Order is a separate step, done from the Inspection Log (see below).

Inspection Log

The Inspection Log shows a searchable history of all submitted inspection reports across all plant. Use it to review previous reports, check a vehicle's inspection history, or follow up on flagged faults.

You can filter the log by plant, date range, and inspection config, and toggle between "Show All" and "Faults & Monitors" only. Tap any row to view the full report including individual question responses and any photos attached.

Turning a fault or monitor into a Work Order

Faults and monitors are not automatically linked to a Work Order when the inspection is submitted — you link them from the Inspection Log:

  • Tap the WO button on an individual fault/monitor row to create or attach it to a Work Order for that plant.
  • Or use the checkboxes to select several faults/monitors at once, then use the action bar at the bottom of the screen to Create Work Order (new or attach to an existing open WO for that plant) or Monitor (batch-downgrade a fault to a monitor item).

Work Orders

Work Orders are job cards for plant maintenance and repairs. The screen opens with three tabs:

Tab What it shows
Open All Work Orders with status Open, In Progress, Scheduled, or Waiting on Parts
Mine Work Orders assigned to you that are not yet completed
Completed The 50 most recently completed Work Orders

Reading the grid

Each row shows the Priority (Low / Normal / High / Urgent), Status, Plant description, Title, Location, Assigned mechanic, Scheduled date, and counts of linked Faults and Tasks.

The WO detail screen's status bar can also show a fifth priority level, Critical, above Urgent.

Filtering the list

  • Type in the Search all columns box to filter across every field at once.
  • Type in a column header's filter box to narrow by that column only.
  • Use the Day / Week / Month chips to filter by scheduled (or completed) date, then use the arrow buttons to step through periods.

Creating a Work Order

  1. Tap + New WO (bottom-right of the screen).
  2. Enter a title, select the plant (use the Filter Plant search box to narrow the dropdown if needed), set the priority and optionally a scheduled date.
  3. If the job has no associated plant (workshop fabrication work), switch on Fabrication Job — this hides the plant picker so you can create the WO without linking it to a vehicle.
  4. Optionally choose a WO Template — this pre-fills a standard task checklist.
  5. Tap Create.

Inside a Work Order

Tap any row in the Work Orders list to open the detail screen. The title bar shows the WO title, plant description, and a unique reference number (e.g. 000042-282-001). A status bar below the title shows status, priority, location, clock reading at creation, and progress counts.

Claiming a Work Order

If a WO is Open and not yet assigned, you will see a Claim WO button. Tapping this assigns it to you and moves the status to In Progress.

Completing a Work Order

  1. Tap Complete WO.
  2. If there are unresolved faults or incomplete tasks, a confirmation dialog is shown — you can proceed or go back to finish them first.
  3. Enter the final clock reading (optional) and tap Complete WO.

Tabs inside the Work Order

The detail screen has six tabs:

Tab What it contains
Faults Faults linked from an inspection report. Update each fault's status using the dropdown (e.g. Repaired, Monitored). Add notes directly on the fault card. Fault count shown as a red badge when faults remain open.
Tasks A checklist of work steps. Tick each task as you go. Tap Add Task to add steps not on the template. Completed tasks are struck through and highlighted green.
Tools Record small plant items needed for the job (e.g. a welder, a torque wrench). Use the search button to pick from the small plant register.
Parts Record consumable parts used. Search the parts inventory by name or barcode, set the quantity and unit. Parts can be linked to a specific fault.
Materials Record workshop materials used (e.g. oils, fluids, consumables). Works the same way as Parts but draws from the materials inventory.
Photos Attach before/after photos of the work. Photos can be linked to a specific fault. Add a caption for each photo.
✅

The status bar at the top of the Work Order screen shows live progress counts — for example "Faults 2/3" means two of three faults have been resolved. Green indicates all items are done.

Vehicle Parts Lists

Vehicle Parts Lists let you store a standard set of parts for a vehicle so they can be bulk-loaded into a Work Order rather than added one by one.

On the main screen you can filter the lists by vehicle. Each list shows the vehicle it belongs to, the number of line items, and optionally a linked WO Template. Tap a list to view and manage its items.

When creating or editing a Work Order, if a matching parts list exists for the selected vehicle, you can load it directly into the Parts tab rather than entering each part manually.

Service Register

The Service Register shows the maintenance status of every registered vehicle and plant item. Each row displays:

  • Plant description
  • Service status — OK (green), Due Soon (orange), or Overdue (red)
  • Last service date and the interval used to calculate the next due date
  • CVRT status — if a CVRT due date is recorded, it shows separately with the same colour coding

The list is sorted with the most urgent items at the top. Use the filter chips (All / Overdue / Due Soon / OK / CVRT) or the search box to narrow the list. Tap any row to view the full service history for that vehicle.

Recording a service

Tap Record Service to log a completed service — choose the service type (Routine / Minor / Major / CVRT / Repair / Other), enter the date and clock reading, note whether it was done externally/by a dealer, and who completed it.

Adding a vehicle to the register

Tap Add to Register to add a new vehicle, setting its vehicle type, registration, and CVRT due date.

Editing a vehicle's config

Open a vehicle and tap the edit (pencil) icon to change its vehicle type, registration, or CVRT due date, and to set per-vehicle service interval overrides (months / km / hours / due-soon thresholds) directly from this screen — you don't need Workshop Admin access to override intervals for an individual vehicle.

ℹ️

Default service intervals for each vehicle type are configured in Workshop Admin → Service Intervals. If a vehicle shows an unexpected status and you can't see why, check whether a per-vehicle override has been set here before contacting your Workshop Manager.

Workshop Dashboard Cards

Workshop users have a Workshop category on their Dashboard. The four cards available in this category give quick access to the most common workshop tasks without needing to navigate the full menu.

Card What it shows / does
Workshop Quick Actions Four large buttons for the fitter workflow: Start Inspection (opens plant picker then jumps to Inspection Report), Service Sheet (opens plant picker for a service record), Quick Job (create a new WO directly from a plant selection), and My Job Cards (opens Work Orders → Mine tab)
My Work Orders Count of open Work Orders assigned to you. Expand the card to see a list of titles and their status. Tap any row to open the WO detail screen.
Open Work Orders Count of all open and in-progress WOs across the workshop. Expand to see a list with titles, locations, priorities, and statuses. Tap any row to open the WO detail screen.
WO Status Counts A summary showing how many Work Orders exist in each status (Open, In Progress, Waiting on Parts, Completed, Cancelled). Useful for a quick workshop overview.

You can toggle individual cards on or off and change their order from the Dashboard gear icon — see the Customising Your Widgets section for details.
