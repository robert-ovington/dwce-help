Timesheets

Your timesheet is made up of individual Time Periods — one for each block of work in a day. This section covers how to add, view, edit and understand your timesheet.

Adding a Time Period

A Time Period is a single record of work — it captures the date, the hours you worked, what project or vehicle you were on, and any additional information such as equipment used, allowances, and materials. You should add a time period for each distinct block of work in your day.

  1. From the main menu, tap Timesheets → New Entry. This opens the time period form directly.
  2. Work through each section of the form (described below).
  3. When all required fields are complete, tap Upload Time Period to review the confirmation popup and confirm.

a. Date, Time & Breaks

This section sets the date and the hours worked for this time period.

Date

Tap the Date field to open the date picker. By default it will be set to today's date. Scroll to select a different date if you are entering a time period for a previous day.

⚠️

Always check the date is correct before saving. Timesheets submitted for the wrong date will need to be corrected by your supervisor.

Start Time & End Time

Tap the Start Time field and use the time picker to set the time you began working. Repeat for End Time when you finished.

The app automatically calculates your total hours based on the start and end times minus any breaks you record.

Breaks

If you took an unpaid break during this time period, record it in the Break field. Enter the total duration of unpaid breaks in minutes. This amount is deducted from your total working hours.

✅

Only enter breaks that were unpaid. If you had a paid lunch break, do not include it here.

b. Project / Fleet

This section links your time period to either a Project (a specific job or contract) or a Fleet item (a vehicle or piece of plant). You will switch between the two depending on the nature of your work.

Switching between Project and Fleet

At the top of this section you will see two tabs or a toggle: Project and Fleet. Tap the one that applies to your work:

  • Project — use this if your time is allocated to a specific site or contract
  • Fleet — use this if your time is allocated to a specific vehicle or machine

Searching and filtering the list

If there are many projects or fleet items, use the Filter / Search bar to type part of the name and narrow down the list quickly.

Find Nearest Job

Tap Find Nearest Job to use your current GPS location to automatically identify and select the closest active project to where you are standing. This is the quickest option when you are already on site.

If there are multiple nearby sites, the button changes to Find Next after the first match. Keep tapping it to cycle through other nearby projects until you find the correct one.

Find Last Job

Tap Find Last Job to review your recent projects one at a time — the dialog shows "Last Job N of M" along with the project, date last used, client name, and description of work. Use Previous or Skip to move to another recent job, or tap Select to fill in the project field with the one shown.

✅

Find Last Job does not use GPS — it pulls from your stored project history. It works offline too.

c. Fleet & Equipment

In addition to your primary project or fleet allocation, you can record any specific vehicles or equipment you operated or used during this time period. The section is split into two lists: Used Fleet and Mobilised Fleet & Equipment.

Adding equipment

Use the numbered search fields (e.g. "Mobilised 1", "Mobilised 2") to add items to the Used Fleet or Mobilised Fleet & Equipment lists — type part of the name to search and select a match.

Saving and reusing a fleet selection

If you regularly use the same set of vehicles or equipment, three buttons help you avoid re-entering them each time:

  • Save Fleet — stores your current selection for reuse
  • Recall Fleet — loads your previously saved selection back into the form
  • Clear Fleet — empties the current selection

Enable Auto Load Saved Fleet to have your saved selection load automatically on future time periods without needing to tap Recall Fleet each time.

Saving your fleet selection with the time period

Your fleet and equipment selection is saved as part of the time period when you tap the main Upload Time Period button at the end of the form. There is no need to save this section separately (Save Fleet is only for reusing the selection on future entries).

d. Allowances

Allowances are additional payments beyond your basic hourly rate. This section covers MISC time and Travel time.

MISC

Use the MISC stepper to record miscellaneous time not directly worked (e.g. a site allowance period). Tap − / + to adjust the value in 30-minute increments, or use the dropdown to pick a value directly.

Travel

Enter travel time using the Travel To Site and Travel From Site minute dropdowns. If you're not sure of the correct amount, tap Check Travel — the app uses GPS to work out a Travel Summary (total travel time, available allowance, rounded one-way/two-way time, and total distance) that you can use to fill in the fields accurately.

ℹ️

If you're unsure what MISC or Travel time to record, or believe the Check Travel result is wrong, contact the office — allowance rules are managed by administration.

e. Materials (Concrete Lorry Drivers only)

The Materials section is only visible to drivers of the concrete mixing lorry. It allows you to record a concrete load as part of your work record.

🔹

If you do not see a Materials section on your timesheet form, this feature has not been enabled for your account. This is expected — it is a driver-specific feature.

Recording materials

Fill in the three fields in the Materials section:

  • Ticket Number — the docket/ticket number for the load
  • Concrete Mix — select the mix type from the dropdown
  • Quantity — enter the amount loaded

This section covers a single load — if you deliver multiple loads, record each as a separate time period.

f. Comments

The Comments field is a free-text area where you can add any relevant notes about this time period. This is optional but can be useful for recording context that is not captured elsewhere in the form.

Examples of useful comments

  • "Concrete pour on foundation — worked alongside groundworks crew"
  • "Plant breakdown from 10:00 to 12:00 — waiting on repairs"
  • "Assisted delivery driver in afternoon"
  • "Site access delayed — started 45 minutes late due to gate issue"
✅

Comments are visible to your supervisor when reviewing timesheets. Keep them factual and professional.

g. Reviewing Before Saving

Before your time period is saved to the system, the app displays a Confirm Time Period Entry popup. This is your opportunity to check all the details are correct before confirming.

What the popup shows

  • Date and day of the week
  • Start time, end time, and total hours worked
  • Break duration
  • Project or fleet item selected
  • Equipment items added
  • MISC and Travel time entered
  • Materials (if applicable)
  • Your comment (if entered)

Confirming or going back

  • If everything is correct, tap OK to submit the time period
  • If something is wrong, tap Cancel to return to the form and make corrections
⚠️

Always review the summary carefully. Once saved, a time period can still be edited, but only while its status is still "Submitted". Once a supervisor or admin approves it, further corrections must be made by them.

Entering a Time Period for Someone Else

If your role allows it (typically supervisors and office/admin staff), two extra checkboxes appear at the top of the time period form so you can enter time on behalf of other people instead of yourself.

Other User

Tick Other User to record a time period for a single employee. Once ticked, a dropdown appears to select the employee. The Times & Breaks, Find Last Job, Saved Fleet, Allowances, and time period summary sections all then relate to that employee, not to you. Untick the box to revert everything back to your own data.

Multiple User

Tick Multiple User instead of Other User to submit the same time period to several employees from one employer at once — useful when a whole crew worked the same hours on the same job.

  1. Tick Multiple User. Selecting an Employer is required before you can add employees.
  2. Use Add Employee to build up a list of employees from that employer. Each added employee appears as a chip — tap the × on a chip to remove them from the list.
  3. Only the Date, Time & Breaks and Project sections are shown — there's no per-employee summary, Fleet, Allowances, Materials or Comments in this mode, since the same entry is applied to everyone in the list.
  4. If you use Find Last Job with more than one employee added, a popup first asks which employee's job history to search.
  5. Tap Upload Time Period to review the preview before it's created for every employee in the list.
⚠️

If any selected employee already has a time period that overlaps the one you're entering, the preview shows a warning and blocks submission entirely — for everyone in the list, not just the employee with the conflict. Cancel, then either adjust the time or remove that employee from the list before trying again.

My Timesheet — Viewing, Editing & Deleting

My Timesheet shows all the time periods you have submitted, organised by date. Use it to check what you have entered, make corrections, or remove incorrect entries.

  1. From the main menu, tap My Timesheet.
  2. Your list of submitted time periods will appear, most recent at the top.
  3. Tap any time period to open its full details.

Time Period Options

Tapping a time period opens a Time Period Options popup with three choices: Edit, Delete, or Cancel.

Editing a Time Period

If you made a mistake on a saved time period, you can edit it as long as its status is still Submitted — that is, before a supervisor or admin has approved it.

  1. Tap the time period you want to edit and choose Edit from the popup.
  2. Make the necessary changes to any field.
  3. Tap Upload Time Period and confirm in the popup.
ℹ️

Once a time period has been approved by a supervisor or admin, editing is blocked and the app tells you who approved it. Contact them to request an amendment.

Deleting a Time Period

If you have added a time period in error (for example, a duplicate entry), you can delete it while its status is still Submitted.

  1. Tap the time period you want to delete and choose Delete from the popup.
  2. A confirmation prompt will appear — confirm to delete permanently.
⚠️

Deletion cannot be undone. Only delete a time period if you are certain it was entered in error.

My Hours

My Hours gives you a simple summary of the hours recorded across your submitted time periods. Use it to check your running total for the week or pay period.

  1. From the main menu, tap My Hours.
  2. The screen displays a summary of your submitted time data grouped by week or pay period.

What My Hours shows

  • Hours per day for the selected week, plus travel (ET) and MISC (MS) minutes
  • A total row for the week
✅

My Hours shows the data you have entered in the app. If a time period is still pending approval it may not yet be reflected in your payroll — check with the office if you have queries about your pay.

Returning to Today's Date

When filling in a time period, if you have navigated to a different date, a Reset to Today button will appear next to the date field. Tap it to jump back to today's date without opening the date picker again.

Time Off

Time Off lets you request leave and see a calendar of your time off and public holidays for the year.

  1. From the main menu, tap Timesheets → Time Off.
  2. The screen shows your time off requests for the current year, plus a month calendar highlighting time-off days and public holidays.
  3. Tap Add Time Off Request to open the request form.
  4. Select the Start Date and End Date, choose the type of leave, and tap Submit.

Each request shows a status badge — Pending, Approved, or Declined — as it moves through approval.
